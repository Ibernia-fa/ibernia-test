warning Account Closure Initiate the post-termination data lifecycle

If you wish to close your account, the following lifecycle applies per our Terms of Service (§8.3):

  1. Export window (60 days): Your account converts to read-only mode. You may download all your data.
  2. Active deletion (30 days): All data is deleted or anonymised from active systems.
  3. Backup removal (up to 90 days): Encrypted backup copies are overwritten.

Please ensure you have exported any records needed for your own regulatory record-keeping obligations before proceeding. This action cannot be easily reversed.
